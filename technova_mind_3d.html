Interactive exercise

Breathe in. Hold. Breathe out.

Use the animated guide for a simple 4–4–6 breathing cycle. Stop if you feel uncomfortable, dizzy, or short of breath.

Follow the shape

When the circle expands, inhale gently through your nose. During “Hold,” stay relaxed rather than straining. As it contracts, exhale slowly. You can pause or restart at any time.
