Your mental health matters

Care can begin small.

You do not need a perfect routine. Tiny actions — a slow breath, asking for help, learning something new, or speaking kindly to yourself — can be meaningful.

Take one minute for you
🌿

Take a breath

Pause for a moment and give your nervous system a little room to settle.

☀️

Notice the good

Look for one small thing that feels safe, steady, useful, or comforting right now.

🤲

Ask for help

Reaching out can be a sign of awareness and strength, not failure.

♡

Be kind to you

Speak to yourself with the same patience you would offer someone you care about.

Guided breathing

Give your mind a little more space.

Try a gentle 4–4–6 pattern: breathe in for four seconds, hold for four, and breathe out for six. Stop if it feels uncomfortable and return to normal breathing.

Cycle: 0 • Inhale 4s • Hold 4s • Exhale 6s
